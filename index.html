SAT Exclusion Criteria
Patient excluded from SAT if any box is checked
End-of-Life / Comfort Care
Goals of care focused on comfort rather than liberation. Awakening may cause unnecessary distress without benefit. MICU Protocol 2025; PADIS Guidelines 2018
Threshold: FiO2 >60%
Safety-driven consensus threshold. Patients requiring >60% FiO2 are at higher risk for hypoxemia during sedation interruption. Eskesen 2018; Grissom 2023; ABC Trial (Girard 2008)
Prone Positioning = SAT Exclusion
Patients require deep sedation during prone positioning to prevent movement, line displacement, and accidental extubation. PROSEVA Trial; MICU Protocol 2025
Cardiac Instability = SAT Exclusion
Awakening increases sympathetic drive and myocardial oxygen demand. Can extend infarct size and precipitate arrhythmias. ABC Trial (Girard 2008); MICU Protocol 2025
Active Seizures = SAT Exclusion
Uncontrolled seizures require ongoing sedation for seizure control. Wait until seizure-free for 24h before SAT attempt. MICU Protocol 2025; Neurocritical Care Guidelines
RASS +3/+4 = SAT Exclusion
Severe agitation poses safety risks. May cause self-extubation, line dislodgement, or injury. Address underlying cause first. PADIS Guidelines 2018; MICU Protocol 2025
Severe Withdrawal = SAT Exclusion
Alcohol/drug withdrawal with autonomic instability requires continued sedation to prevent seizures and CV complications. CIWA Protocol; MICU Protocol 2025
Uncontrolled Pain (CPOT ≥3) = SAT Exclusion
Pain must be addressed before stopping sedation. Optimize analgesia first (analgesia-first approach per PADIS). PADIS Guidelines 2018; MICU Protocol 2025
CRITICAL: NMB = Absolute SAT Contraindication
Stopping sedation while paralyzed causes awareness without ability to move — severe psychological trauma. Wait ≥4h after NMB stopped. MICU Protocol 2025; PADIS Guidelines 2018
Elevated ICP = SAT Exclusion
Agitation and coughing during awakening sharply increase ICP. Risk of herniation. Reassess when ICP <20 mmHg stable for 24h. Neurocritical Care Guidelines; MICU Protocol 2025
TTM = SAT Exclusion
Targeted temperature management requires sedation to prevent shivering. Reassess 24h after normothermia achieved. TTM Guidelines; MICU Protocol 2025
Procedure Today = Defer SAT
Patient will need re-sedation for procedure. Starting then stopping SAT is unnecessarily disruptive. Defer to next day. MICU Protocol 2025

Resolution of Primary Pathology
SBT should only be attempted when initial reason for intubation is resolving. Is pneumonia improving? Pulmonary edema resolving? Sepsis controlled? Boles 2007; ATS/CHEST Guidelines 2017; MICU Protocol 2025
Airway Protection Assessment
Weak/absent cough (negative white card test) strongly predicts extubation failure. Indicates inability to clear secretions and protect airway. Elkholy 2021; Saltürk 2016; Thille 2016

High-Risk Extubation Factors
Determines post-extubation support level
Risk Stratification:
0 factors = Standard O₂ | 1-3 factors = HFNC or NIV | ≥4 factors = NIV strongly recommended Hernández 2016; Thille 2019; ERS Guidelines
Age >65: Established Risk Factor
Advanced age consistently predicts extubation failure — decreased respiratory muscle strength and reduced physiologic reserve. Thille 2015; Hernández 2016; Villamizar 2025
BMI ≥30: WHO Obesity
Obesity affects lung mechanics, airway patency, and increases respiratory work. De Jong 2024; Pensier 2024; Thille 2021
Heart Failure: Strong Predictor
Severe LV dysfunction independently associated with failure. Removal of positive pressure may precipitate pulmonary edema. Thille 2015; Villamizar 2025; Rady 1999
COPD: High-Risk Population
Failure rates 14.7%-52% in COPD (vs 5-15% general ICU). Prophylactic NIV strongly beneficial. Elkholy 2021; Robriquet 2006; Goharani 2019
Prolonged MV ≥7 days
Defines "prolonged weaning" per ERS/WIND. Associated with respiratory muscle deconditioning. Trudzinski 2022; Schönhofer 2020
≥2 Failed SBTs: Difficult Weaning
Multiple failures indicate underlying respiratory insufficiency or unresolved pathology. Boles 2007; Torrini 2021
Suctioning >Q2h = HIGH RISK
High secretion burden (>12×/24h) strongly predicts failure with OR 10.65. Haruna 2022; Hattori 2025
Weak Cough: Airway Protection Risk
Weak/absent cough is significant predictor of failure. Indicates inability to protect airway. Elkholy 2021; Saltürk 2016; Thille 2016
Fluid Balance Impact:
>+1000 mL/24h = high specificity (84%) for failure. Consider diuresis optimization. Wang 2024; Maezawa 2019
